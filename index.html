How we use estate agents’ business contact details

This notice explains why you may have received an email from us, where we found your details and what you can do about it. It applies to estate agencies in the United Kingdom.

Who we are

WebHunter s.r.o., a company registered in the Czech Republic (Company ID 29498511), Prague, Czech Republic. We build presentation websites for property listings.

Contact for anything in this notice: info.webhunter@email.cz

What we collect and where from

We only use business information that estate agencies publish themselves:

  • the agency’s name, branch, business email address, phone number, website and company registration details;
  • the public details of a property the agency advertises (description, photos, price, location), taken from the public listing on a property portal such as OnTheMarket;
  • the business email address and company details shown on the agency’s own website, which the listing links to.

We do not buy contact lists and we do not collect personal addresses.

Why we use it

To prepare a draft presentation website for a property you advertise and to send you one email offering it to you. If you reply, we use your details to answer you and, if you agree, to set up the website.

Our lawful basis

Legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): offering a relevant business service to a business about a property it is actively marketing. We contact incorporated businesses only (limited companies, LLPs and PLCs), as permitted by regulation 22 of the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations. Every email identifies us and tells you how to opt out.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your details. We use service providers to run the service: an email delivery provider (Resend) to send our messages and GitHub Pages to host the draft websites. Some of these providers are based outside the UK; where that is the case, transfers are covered by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or an equivalent safeguard.

How long we keep it

If you do not reply, we delete your contact details within 12 months of our email. If you ask us not to contact you, we keep only your email address on a suppression list so that we never contact you again. Draft websites are removed on request at any time.

Your rights

  • Object and opt out: reply “unsubscribe” to any email and you will not hear from us again.
  • Access, correction and deletion: ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Remove a draft website: tell us and we will take it down.
  • Complain: you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

We do not use your details for automated decision-making or profiling.
